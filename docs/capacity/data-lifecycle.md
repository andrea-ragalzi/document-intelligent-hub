demo. It does not assume storage-provider pricing, quota, TTL, or lifecycle
features that are not configured in this repository.

## Configured limits

- Personal uploads: limits follow the authenticated tier; UNLIMITED has no product count or size limit.
- Parsed text: 500,000 characters per PDF.
- Chunks and embeddings: 500 per PDF, 2,500 personal chunks per user.
- Persisted conversations: 20 per user, 40 messages and 12,000 message-text characters per conversation.

The ingestion limits are configured in the backend tier/indexing constants. The
conversation limits are enforced in `frontend/lib/conversationsService.ts`, and
the 40-message structural ceiling is duplicated in `frontend/firestore.rules`.
